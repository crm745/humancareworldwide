Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can an air ambulance be dispatched?

For critical cases, our team can have an aircraft airborne within 2–4 hours of confirmed clearance. Response time depends on aircraft positioning and destination overflight permits.
Will my insurance / TPA cover the cost?

We work directly with most major TPAs and insurers on a Guarantee of Payment (GOP) basis. Share your policy number in the request form and our team will coordinate approval directly with your insurer.
How do I track my request status?

Use "Track My Request" on the Home screen with your reference number and registered mobile number — no account needed. Signed-in patients can also see full live status under their Account tab.
What documents do I need to submit?

A recent medical report or fit-to-fly certificate speeds up clearance. You can attach it directly in the request form, or send it later once our coordinator contacts you.
Do you operate in my country?

We coordinate transport across 168 countries through our network of 3,500+ partner hospitals. Submit a request from anywhere and our global desk will confirm coverage for your route.
