Frequently Asked Questions
How fast can an air ambulance be dispatched?

For critical cases, our team can have an aircraft airborne within 2–4 hours of confirmed clearance.

We work directly with most major TPAs and insurers on a Guarantee of Payment basis.

How do I track my request status?

Open My Requests, tap a request, then View Live Tracking — no separate app needed.
